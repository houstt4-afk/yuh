    How this works
    
    
      
  • Base64 writes every byte of the file as text. It can rebuild the exact image, which is why a phone photo runs to millions of characters.
    • 
      
  • Fingerprint: the photo is turned grayscale and averaged down to 32×32, a DCT pulls out the 8×8 coarsest patterns, and each one becomes a 1 or 0 depending on whether it's above the median. Resizing, recompressing, or small edits barely change it.
    • 
      
  • Fingerprints here can differ by a few bits from the Python script, because the browser shrinks images a little differently than Pillow does.
    • 
      
  • To find a photo on the internet, use Google Lens or TinEye. They run this same kind of fingerprint against billions of indexed images.
